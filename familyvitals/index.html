Read at a glance一眼看懂

See how far out of range — not just that it is. 不只告诉你异常，还告诉你偏离多少。

Every result is drawn against the reference range printed on its own report, so a value just over the line looks different from one far outside it. 每一项指标都画在它自己那张报告印的参考范围上：刚刚超出一点，和远远超出，一眼就分得清。

  • ↓ Blue with a down arrow is low.蓝色加向下箭头是偏低。
  • ↑ Red with an up arrow is high or positive.红色加向上箭头是偏高或阳性。
  • – Normal stays quiet. No color, so what matters stands out. Arrows mean it never depends on color alone.正常的不上色，需要留意的才会跳出来。有箭头，所以不靠颜色也能看懂。

Features功能

Built for the reports you actually get.为你手里真实的报告而做。

Clinic printouts, hospital PDFs, a photo of a page on the kitchen table — in English or Chinese.诊所打印件、医院 PDF、随手拍的一页纸，中文英文都行。

Scan on your iPhone手机上直接识别

Scan paper or pick photos and PDFs. Dates, hospitals and lab values are read on the device — free, offline, no key.扫描纸质报告或选照片、PDF，在手机上识别日期、医院和数值。免费、不联网、不要 Key。

Trends with sources趋势可追溯

Open any test for its chart and every past result, each linked back to the report it came from.点开任意指标看趋势图和历次结果，每个数都连回来源报告。

Overview first先看重点

Opens on what needs attention and when the next follow-up is, not on a wall of numbers.打开先看哪些要留意、下次复查是什么时候，而不是一堆数字。

Meds & follow-ups用药与复查

Medication list, follow-up reminders, and a one-page visit prep sheet to show the doctor.用药清单、复查提醒，还有一页给医生看的就诊准备清单。

The whole family全家人

A separate record for each person — parents, kids, yourself. Switch with one tap; reports never mix.每位家人单独建档：父母、孩子、自己。一键切换，报告不会混。

One search全局搜索

Reports, results, hospitals and medications, from any tab.在任何页签都能搜报告、指标、医院和用药。

Imaging side by side影像报告对比

Line up ultrasound and imaging reports of the same area to see what changed between visits.同一部位的超声、影像报告并排对比，看看两次之间有什么变化。

Light, dark, 中文深浅色，中英文

Follows your iPhone’s appearance, including dark at night. Fully in English or Chinese.跟随系统深浅色（包括夜间自动深色），中文、English 完整切换。

Privacy隐私

Your health records stay yours.你的病历，只属于你。

There is no FamilyVitals server. Nothing about you reaches the developer — there is nowhere for it to go.FamilyVitals 没有服务器。开发者收不到你的任何数据——根本没有地方可去。

  • On your iPhone only只存在你的 iPhone 上No account, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking.不用账号、不用登录，没有广告和追踪。
  • Optional app lock可选应用锁Face ID, Touch ID or passcode, and the screen is hidden in the app switcher.Face ID、Touch ID 或密码；切换 App 时画面会被遮住。
  • AI only if you turn it onAI 只在你打开时使用It asks your consent for each provider. Names, ID numbers and contact details are removed on the phone first; original files are never uploaded.每个服务商都会先征求你同意。姓名、证件号、联系方式先在手机上去掉，原始文件不会上传。
  • Yours to take with you随时带走Export a full backup any time and import it on a new iPhone.随时导出完整备份，换手机直接导入。

Optional AI reading可选 AI 读取

Bring your own key for the hard reports.复杂报告，用你自己的 Key。
